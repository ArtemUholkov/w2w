What Is WAVi?

qEEG pairs FDA-cleared electroencephalography (EEG) hardware with advanced analytics to create a comprehensive brain map, much like an EKG for the heart.

Easy-to-read reports compare your metrics against age-matched norms and your own historical scans, establishing a cognitive baseline for lifelong brain-health tracking.

How Does It Work?

  • Setup

    Setup

    A lightweight headset with 10–20 EEG sensors is placed on your scalp; electrodes are moistened with 0.9 % saline for crisp signal quality. Total prep time: <2 min.

  • Recording

    Recording

    Relax or complete brief auditory / memory tasks while WAVi records electrical brain activity and event-related potentials.

  • Analysis

    Analysis

    Proprietary software converts raw data into an intuitive dashboard - highlighting strengths, opportunities, and trends you can act on immediately.

  • Scan time:

    4–15 minutes

  • Full visit:

    ≈ 30–45 minutes (including set-up & review)

  • Downtime:

    None

Contra-Indications & Cautions

  • qEEGis not a diagnostic device; results must be interpreted by a qualified clinician
  • Severe scalp irritation or open wounds may impede headset placement
  • Clients with known epilepsy should inform staff prior to scanning

Post-Scan: What to Expect

  • Resume normal activity immediately - no side-effects, no radiation exposure
  • Review results on the spot with a provider and receive personalised recommendations
  • Follow-up scans may be scheduled to measure the impact of therapies or lifestyle changes
