What is VO₂ Max Testing?

VO₂ Max Testing is an advanced cardiovascular assessment that measures your body’s maximum oxygen uptake. It provides valuable insights into your oxygen efficiency, metabolic health, and overall fitness.

What are the benefits of VO₂ Max Testing?

  • Optimizes your fitness routine
  • Enhances endurance and athletic performance
  • Identifies your maximum fat-burning potential

How does VO₂ Max Testing work?

During the test, you will perform cardiovascular exercise (such as running or cycling) while wearing a mask that measures the oxygen you inhale and the carbon dioxide you exhale. This data is analyzed to determine your VO₂ Max, or maximum oxygen uptake.

How long does the test take?

The testing session typically lasts 30–60 minutes, including preparation, the test itself, and initial review of results.
