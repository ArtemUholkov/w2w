What is Hyperbaric Therapy?

Hyperbaric Therapy is a non-invasive wellness treatment that involves breathing 100% pure oxygen in a pressurized chamber. This process enhances the body’s natural healing abilities by increasing oxygen delivery to your blood, tissues, and cells.

How does Hyperbaric Therapy work?

Inside the hyperbaric chamber, the air pressure is increased to levels higher than normal atmospheric pressure. This allows your lungs to absorb significantly more oxygen, which is then distributed throughout your body to promote healing and recovery.
