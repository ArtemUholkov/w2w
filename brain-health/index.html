What is MeRT™ (Magnetic EEG-Guided Resonance Therapy)?

MeRT™ is a patented, non-invasive therapy that uses targeted magnetic stimulation to identify and treat specific areas of the brain. By applying magnetic pulses guided by EEG (electroencephalogram) readings, it aims to improve a wide range of neurological conditions.

What conditions can MeRT™ help treat?

MeRT™ has shown effectiveness in treating various neurological conditions, including:

  • Depression
  • PTSD (Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder)
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder
  • Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)
  • Anxiety
  • Chronic Pain
  • Sleep Disorders And many more conditions related to brain health.

How long does a MeRT™ session take?

A typical MeRT™ session lasts between 30 to 45 minutes. Treatment frequency and duration vary depending on the specific condition being addressed and individual patient needs.

How many sessions will I need?

The number of sessions required varies from person to person, depending on the condition and response to treatment. Most patients begin to see improvements within 10-20 sessions, but a personalized treatment plan will be created based on your brain health assessment.
