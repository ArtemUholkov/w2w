Any questions?

How does the scan work?

Our technology uses dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) or similar gold-standard methods to deliver highly accurate measurements of your body’s composition.

What are the benefits of body composition scanning?

  • Accurate measurement of fat, muscle, and bone mass
  • Insight into fat distribution and muscle imbalances
  • Identification of changes in bone density
  • Data to guide fitness, nutrition, and health plans

How long does a scan take?

The scanning process typically takes 10–20 minutes.

How often should I get a body composition scan?

It depends on your goals. For tracking progress in fitness or weight management, every 3–6 months is recommended.
