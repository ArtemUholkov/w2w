What is Red Light Therapy?

Red Light Therapy (RLT) uses specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light to penetrate the skin and stimulate cellular repair, regeneration, and overall wellness.

How does Red Light Therapy work?

Red and near-infrared light are absorbed by the mitochondria, the powerhouse of cells. This stimulates energy production (ATP), enhancing cellular repair and reducing inflammation.
