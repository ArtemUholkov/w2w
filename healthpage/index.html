Hormone Changes As You Age

For men, testosterone levels decline starting at age 35, with 40% over 45 experiencing low levels, affecting energy, strength, sexual performance and body fat.

For women, 47% aged 30-60 have hormonal imbalances, often undetected until later, causing fatigue, reduced sex drive and weight issues.
